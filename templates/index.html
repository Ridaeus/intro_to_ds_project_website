01 — THE PROBLEM

Unfamiliar indoor spaces are hard to navigate

Who can deny that visiting new places is frustrating at best, and wholly disorienting at worst? Existing tools help in their own ways, but none of them give you spatial awareness.

01

Tactile maps

Helpful, but placed sparingly, if present at all.

02

Recognition apps

Great at telling you what is nearby, reading product descriptions and naming people.

03

What's missing

Neither is designed to guide you through buildings and rooms to your destination.

02 — OUR SOLUTION

Depth perception meets spatial audio

[Product name] uses an infrared depth camera to detect important landmarks in indoor spaces, such as doors and railings. It tells you where they are through sound marks, building the environment around you in sound.

  1. Step 1

    Detect The camera spots landmarks like doors and railings.

  2. Step 2

    Locate It works out where each landmark is and how close it is to you.

  3. Step 3

    Hear Each sound mark changes with the landmark's distance and direction.

03 — AN EXAMPLE

Meeting a friend in a new library

Imagine walking into a library you have never visited. There is no map at the entrance, but you came prepared: someone mapped out the route for you beforehand.

After leaving the elevator, you walk straight through one door. Then you take the first right turn and keep walking past three meeting cubicles until you reach the open area, where you will meet your friend.

ALONG THE WAY, [PRODUCT NAME] CONFIRMS

  1. You are walking towards a door.
  2. You took the correct right turn.
  3. You just passed the three cubicle doors in a row.
  4. While you wait, the cubicle doors help you tell which way you are facing.

04 — IN PRACTICE

No new equipment needed

[Product name] does not exist yet, but it would not need any new hardware. Modern smartphones already have infrared cameras and can run this kind of AI.

  1. 01

    Download the app

    Everything runs on the phone you already own.

  2. 02

    Put on headphones

    High-quality stereo headphones give the best experience, but budget-friendly ones work well too.

  3. 03

    Hold your phone steady

    Point it ahead and let the sound build the room around you.

For the curious: what happens behind the scenes?

An AI image detection algorithm works together with data from the infrared depth camera. Together they recognise what kind of object is in front of you, where it is, and how close it is.
